Three decks from the same notes, none of them average.

Paste your notes, say what the presentation is for, and choose between three directions that differ in story and look. Every slide stays editable when you export it.

Preview limitation: the AI step is not connected yet (that's step 2), so the decks below always use the sample content on the Great Vowel Shift. Your text stays in this box for later.

Your own photos are used in the decks. Without them, the preview uses public-domain pictures from Wikimedia Commons, credited in Works Cited. Pictures stay on your device.
